the caribbean digital XII

December 4–5, 2025

Northeastern University | Boston, MA



The Caribbean Digital conference (TCD) is headed to Boston! TCD is excited to be in the Northeast this year, bringing our community to The University of Northeastern’s Boston campus. This year, the conference will be hosted in collaboration with the Early Caribbean Digital Archive. TCD is an annual international gathering held in locations throughout the Americas since 2014. The conference brings together scholars, artists, archivists, librarians, technologists, and activists working at the intersection of Caribbean Studies and digital humanities. Alternating between events organized via CFP and smaller events convened exclusively by invitation, TCD has created a singular and consistent space of exchange for Caribbeanist digital scholars, consisting of traditional panels, roundtable conversations, skills-building workshops, and project show-and-tells.

The wide-ranging work of this gathering has generated nearly 50 peer-reviewed scholarly articles (in archipelagos journal alone) and has inspired and contributed to many digital projects in Caribbean Studies. In addition, the conference has produced its own constellations of digital projects, including a collectively generated bibliography, a collective translation, a keywords project, and a directory of Caribbean digital scholarship.
